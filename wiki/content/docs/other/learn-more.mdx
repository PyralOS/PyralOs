## FAQ

<details>
    <summary>Which Linux distro will the OS be based on?</summary>

    PyralOS is based off Debian, specifically the AARCH64 architecture, since most phones are like that.

    _(We like x86 but there isn't x86 on phones...)_

</details>

<details>
    <summary>Android already exists, why making another mobile OS?</summary>

    Well, a few reasons:
    1. Google is actively locking down Android, as shown [here](https://keepandroidopen.org);
    2. Even on the "stockest" Android, Google apps are still installed and can track you;
        - Our goal is to keep all your data private, local, and not track everywhere you go
    3. Some parts of Android that you can find in Google phones such as Pixels aren't available on stock Android or the Android version that other phone manufacturers depend on.

</details>
